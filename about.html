01 / Our mission

Make the preparation journey easier to navigate.

UPSC गुरुकुल brings learning resources and practice experiences together in one digital space for aspirants who want to study with greater clarity and consistency.

Preparing for a competitive examination is more than collecting notes. Aspirants also need to understand concepts, plan their study, practise questions, revisit difficult topics and keep track of their progress.

UPSC गुरुकुल is being developed to support that journey through a collection of learning resources, tests, revision experiences and digital tools.

Our approach is straightforward: useful learning tools, clear navigation and an experience that respects the effort every aspirant puts into preparation.

03 / The founder

Built with an aspirant's perspective.

A personal initiative focused on making digital learning resources more useful for UPSC preparation.

Pratyush Swain Founder · UPSC गुरुकुल
The person behind the project

Pratyush Swain

Founder of UPSC गुरुकुल

Pratyush Swain is the founder of UPSC गुरुकुल and a Physics undergraduate with an interest in technology, digital learning and public-service preparation.

His vision for the platform is to bring study resources, practice tools and learning support into a more accessible digital experience for UPSC aspirants.

Rather than presenting preparation as one enormous task, the platform aims to help learners break it into clearer steps: learn a concept, practise it, review the result and keep moving forward.

04 / What guides us

Principles before promises.

Our direction is shaped by practical values, not exaggerated claims.

Useful over complicated

Learning tools should help aspirants study, not make the process harder to navigate.

Progress over perfection

Consistent practice and revision matter. Every improvement to the platform should support that journey.

Clarity and responsibility

Be clear about what a feature does, how a resource is used and where users can ask for help.

05 / Our direction

A platform that keeps learning.

UPSC गुरुकुल is an evolving project. The work continues through improvements to its learning resources, usability and digital tools.

Build the learning foundation

Improve the study experience

Keep listening and improving
