Important: This policy describes general practices for the website and related learning features. Actual data handling depends on the features you use and the services currently configured. Please review it before publication and update it to match your live technical setup.

1. Overview

UPSC गुरुकुल is an educational platform intended to support people preparing for competitive examinations through learning resources, practice activities and related digital features.

This policy explains the categories of information that may be processed, the purposes for processing, and choices that may be available. By using the platform, you acknowledge this notice. Where consent is legally required, this notice alone does not replace obtaining that consent.

2. Information we may handle

  • Account information: details you provide when registering or signing in, such as an email address, display name or authentication identifiers, depending on the sign-in method.
  • Learning activity: quiz answers, scores, progress, saved preferences and other activity where a feature records it.
  • Messages and support: information you include when contacting us or submitting feedback.
  • AI-assisted features: prompts, questions or content you submit to an AI-enabled feature. Avoid including passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information.
  • Device and technical data: browser or device information, diagnostic events, security logs and approximate usage information where the website or its providers make these available.
  • Notification information: a device or messaging token and notification preferences if you enable supported push notifications.

We do not intend to request sensitive personal information for ordinary study activities. Information actually collected depends on the services and settings enabled.

3. How information may be used

  • To provide sign-in, learning resources, tests and saved progress.
  • To maintain the platform, troubleshoot errors and protect accounts and services.
  • To respond to questions, feedback or support requests.
  • To deliver notifications when you enable them and the relevant feature is available.
  • To operate AI-assisted tools when you choose to use them.
  • To understand service performance and improve usability, subject to applicable settings and requirements.
  • To comply with legal obligations and address misuse or security incidents.

We do not promise that every feature described here is enabled at all times.

4. Storage and third-party providers

The platform may use third-party infrastructure for hosting, authentication, databases, notifications or AI-enabled functionality. For example, Firebase services may be used for some application functions if configured. The providers actually used depend on the deployed version.

Those providers may process information under their own terms and privacy documentation. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services. Review their notices before using them.

Information may be processed or stored in locations outside your state or country, depending on the provider's infrastructure and configuration, subject to applicable law.

5. Cookies and local device storage

The website may use browser storage, such as local storage, session storage or cookies, to remember preferences, maintain functionality, support consent choices or improve security. Your browser may allow you to clear or restrict some storage, but doing so can affect website features.

Third-party embedded content or services may use their own technologies if enabled. Optional analytics or advertising should only be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements and the site's actual configuration.

6. Push notifications

If you enable notifications, the platform or your browser may process a notification token and related settings to deliver messages. You can usually disable browser notifications through your browser or device settings. Turning them off may not automatically remove a token already stored by the service; contact us if you need assistance with a request.

Notification delivery depends on device permissions, network access and provider availability.

7. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide a feature, maintain records, resolve disputes, protect the platform or meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the services involved. We do not claim that every record is automatically deleted when an account is closed.

8. Your choices and requests

  • Keep your account details accurate where account settings permit.
  • Manage browser storage and notification permissions using your device or browser settings.
  • Avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information in prompts, feedback or public areas.
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests. We may need to verify your request and may retain some information where the law permits or requires it.

Available rights and response requirements depend on applicable law and the circumstances of your request.

9. Children and educational use

The platform is designed for examination preparation. If you are not legally able to provide consent to a particular form of data processing, involve a parent or legal guardian where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the platform, its providers or applicable requirements change. The updated date at the top indicates the latest stated revision. Please review this page periodically.
